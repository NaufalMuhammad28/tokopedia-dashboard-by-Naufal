### 🛒 About The Project
**Tokopedia Market Intelligence Platform** is an empirical spatial and sales performance analysis system designed to process **29,000+ modern retail e-commerce transactions**. 

This platform bridges big data analytics with retail business strategy, uncovering consumer purchasing patterns, geographical sales density, and price elasticity across diverse product categories.

---

### 📊 Key Features & Analytics
1. **Spatial Sales Density:** Mapping transaction clusters and geographical market penetration to identify high-demand regions.
2. **Retail Performance Metrics:** Evaluating revenue drivers, top-performing product categories, and store performance indicators.
3. **Interactive Dashboard:** A web-based interactive interface designed for stakeholders to filter data dynamically and extract fast business insights.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Data Processing & Analysis:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `SQL`
- **Geospatial Intelligence:** `GeoPandas`, `Spatial Clustering`
- **Dashboard & Web Deployment:** `HTML5`, `CSS3`, `JavaScript`, `GitHub Pages`
